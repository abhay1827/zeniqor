Understanding CAD

What is CAD Designing?

CAD designing in the jewelry field is the process of creating highly detailed 3D digital models of jewelry pieces using specialized computer software before they are physically produced.

A designer builds a piece (ring, band, pendant, earrings, bracelet, etc.) on the computer with exact specifications:

  • 📏
    Dimensions
    Length, width, thickness in mm
  • 💎
    Stone Details
    Stone sizes and placement
  • 🔩
    Setting Details
    Prong, bezel, or setting specifications
  • ⚖️
    Weight Estimation
    Accurate metal weight calculation
  • ✨
    Surface Finish
    Surface finish and structural support

05

The CAD file is used to create a wax/resin model (via 3D printing) for casting in metal.

Jewelry Production Workflow

The complete back-end production process — from casting to shipment and margin reporting.

1

Casting Purchase & Entry

Record the casting purchase in the system. Update casting details in the job bag. Cross-check and verify the casting cost for accuracy.

2

Diamond Entry

Enter and record all diamond specifications and allocation details for each production job.

3

Location Update

Update job location status from Office to Contractor once the job is outsourced for manufacturing.

4

Contractor Invoice Verification

Cross-check the contractor's labor invoice with job details. Ensure charges are accurate and aligned with agreed rates.

5

Quality Check (QC) Approval

Perform final quality inspection. Confirm the piece meets required standards before proceeding.

6

Customer Invoice / Memo Generation

Generate customer invoice or memo after QC approval. Ensure all details (product, pricing, stones, metal) are correct.

7

Shipment Label Generation

Create and attach shipment labels. Verify shipping details and customer address.

8

Post-Shipment Update

Update the production file/system after dispatch. Mark the job as completed/shipped.

9

Margin Report Update

Calculate and update the final margin for the job. Ensure all costs (casting, diamonds, labor) are correctly considered.
